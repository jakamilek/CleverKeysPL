# Polish city-source policy — 2026-09-24

## Official source

The city source is derived at CI time from the current official GUS TERYT / SIMC full catalog.
SIMC is the official system of identifiers and names of localities. The current catalog published by GUS is valid from 2026-01-01 and contains 1,026 localities whose type is `miasto`.

## Extraction rule

- source: GUS TERYT SIMC;
- locality kind: `RM=96` (`miasto`);
- one-token Polish alphabetic names are eligible for the flat CKDT word layer;
- multiword city names are not automatically flattened into a full phrase key;
- recognized hyphenated city names are an explicit exception: the full hyphenated surface is retained alongside its components and is eligible for the flat CKDT layer;
- no name is truncated or altered merely because it is multi-component.

## Base-form policy

Every eligible official one-token city receives its canonical capitalized nominative surface in the Polish pack.
The exact SIMC archive, state date, SHA256 and extraction report are published as CI artifacts.

## Inflection policy

Full singular inflection is generated for:

1. the top 300 eligible one-token city names by Polish wordfreq frequency;
2. explicitly reviewed priority cities, currently including Gdynia and the city names already used as proper-noun regression targets;
3. every official city name containing a recognized lexical hyphen, using a city-specific grammatical model.

Only forms actually generated by Morfeusz 2 / SGJP are accepted. Endings are never invented manually.

## 100k budget

City base forms and selected city inflections are treated as protected reviewed candidates, together with the selected first-name layer and reviewed morphology.
If protected layers alone would exceed the configured dictionary limit, CI fails rather than silently discarding protected forms.

The size-study workflow builds 50k, 75k and 100k variants with the same city and first-name layers, so the effect on the 100k budget is measured rather than guessed.

## Gdynia regression

`Gdynia` is an explicit priority city. The CI gate requires at least the canonical base form and common singular forms:

`Gdynia`, `Gdyni`, `Gdynię`, `Gdynią`.

## Provenance

GUS states that SIMC contains official locality names and that the locality type is a necessary part of correct catalog implementation. The project therefore keeps SIMC identifiers in the generated staging artifact even though CKDT stores only the language surface.

Source documentation:

This policy does not authorize promotion to `main`.
